Lift Supply & Installation Across London, Kent & the South East

We supply and install stairlifts, platform lifts, homelifts and ceiling hoists for residential and commercial properties across London, Kent and the South East of England.

All installations are carried out by our own qualified engineers, ensuring a professional finish, safe commissioning and full demonstration on completion.

Stairlift Installation

We supply and install:

  • Straight stairlifts
  • Curved stairlifts
  • Powered options including swivel seats and footrests

Every installation begins with a detailed site survey carried out by an experienced engineer. We assess measurements, power supply requirements and any structural considerations before ordering equipment.

Platform Lift & Homelift Installation

We install vertical platform lifts, inclined platform lifts and through-floor homelifts in both domestic and commercial settings.

Our installation process includes:

  • Site survey and measurement
  • Manufacturer drawings and approvals
  • Preparation works where required
  • Safe installation and commissioning
  • Full user demonstration

We work closely with property owners, builders and facilities managers to ensure installations are completed safely and efficiently.

Ceiling Hoist Installation

We supply and install ceiling hoists and tracking systems for private homes, care environments and healthcare settings.

Installations are carried out with careful consideration to ceiling structure and load requirements to ensure long-term reliability and safety.
