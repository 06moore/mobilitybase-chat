LOLER Examinations for Lifts & Hoists Across London, Kent & the South East

Under the Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations (LOLER), certain lifts and lifting equipment must be thoroughly examined at regular intervals by a competent person.

We provide independent LOLER examinations across London, Kent and the South East for stairlifts, platform lifts, homelifts and ceiling hoists in both residential and commercial settings.

What is a LOLER Examination?

A LOLER examination is a statutory thorough inspection carried out to ensure lifting equipment is safe to use and compliant with current regulations.

Unlike routine servicing, a LOLER inspection focuses on safety, structural integrity and legal compliance rather than maintenance.

Following examination, a written report and certificate is issued outlining:

  • The condition of the equipment
  • Any defects found
  • Whether the lift remains safe to use
  • Any remedial actions required

Equipment We Examine

We carry out LOLER examinations on:

  • Platform lifts (vertical and inclined)
  • Through-floor homelifts
  • Ceiling hoists and tracking systems
  • Stairlifts where required in commercial environments

Inspection Intervals

Typical LOLER inspection frequencies are:

  • Every 6 months for equipment used to lift people
  • Every 6 months for ceiling hoists
  • Every 12 months for certain lifting equipment depending on use

We can advise on the correct inspection schedule for your premises.
