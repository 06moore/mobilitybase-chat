Before You Call — Try These First

Many lift faults can be resolved quickly without an engineer visit. Before calling us, please check the following:

  • Check the key switch — remove the key, wait 10 seconds, then reinsert and try again.
  • Check the power socket — make sure the plug hasn't been knocked out or switched off at the wall.
  • Check the charging strip — on stairlifts, ensure the carriage is sitting correctly on the charging point at the top or bottom of the rail.
  • Check for obstructions — most lifts have safety sensors that stop the lift if something is blocking the rail or platform.
  • Check the remote controls — try using the controls on the lift itself rather than the remote handset.
  • Check the circuit breaker — if the lift has suddenly lost all power, check your fuse box for a tripped breaker.

If none of the above resolves the fault, call us on 0208 936 3060 and one of our team will talk you through the next steps.

Common Faults We Repair

Our engineers regularly diagnose and repair a wide range of faults across all lift types. Common repairs we carry out include:

  • Stairlift won't move in either direction
  • Lift stops mid-rail or mid-travel
  • Beeping, error codes or warning lights
  • Battery not charging or draining quickly
  • Charger and power supply faults
  • Remote handset not responding
  • Seat swivel mechanism not operating
  • Trailing cable replacements
  • Roller and carriage wheel wear
  • Safety sensor and limit switch faults
  • PCB and controller diagnostics
  • Motor, gearbox and brake unit faults
  • Door lock and interlock failures on platform lifts and homelifts
  • Track alignment and mechanical adjustments
  • Ceiling hoist motor and track faults

Where parts are not immediately available, we will advise clearly on lead times and costs before proceeding. We never carry out work without your approval.
