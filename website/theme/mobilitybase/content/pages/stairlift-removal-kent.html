Stairlift Removal FAQs

How long does stairlift removal take?

Most straight stairlift removals take around 1 hour from start to finish. Curved stairlifts typically take 1–2 hours depending on the complexity of the rail system. Our engineer will always advise you on timing before starting.

Do you remove stairlifts you didn't install?

Yes — absolutely. We remove all makes and models regardless of who originally installed them. Acorn, Stannah, Handicare, Brooks, Thyssen or any other brand — we can remove it safely.

Will removal damage my staircase or walls?

No. Our engineers take care to protect your walls, skirting boards and flooring throughout. Your staircase will be left clean and ready to use when the job is done.

Can you remove a stairlift that has stopped working?

Yes. A faulty or broken stairlift is no more difficult to remove than a working one. We don't need the unit to be operational to carry out the removal.

How quickly can you come out?

We aim for same-week appointments across Kent. For urgent situations — property sales or bereavements — get in touch directly and we'll prioritise your booking wherever possible.

What happens to the stairlift once it's removed?

Once removed we assess the unit. Where it's in good condition we look to rehome it where possible. Where it's beyond use we arrange responsible disposal. We never simply leave you to deal with it — removal means we take it away completely.

Do you cover my area in Kent?

We cover the whole of Kent including Maidstone, Canterbury, Tunbridge Wells, Medway, Folkestone, Dover, Margate, Dartford and everywhere in between. If you're unsure, get in touch and we'll confirm.

How much does stairlift removal cost?

Straight stairlift removal starts from £174 and curved stairlift removal starts from £270. All prices are confirmed upfront before any work begins — no hidden fees.

Do you remove all brands of stairlift?

Yes. We remove all major brands including Acorn, Stannah, Handicare, Brooks, Thyssen, Companion and Minivator — even if we didn't install it originally.

Do you remove stairlifts ahead of a property sale?

Yes — this is one of the most common reasons people contact us. We understand property sales move fast and we'll work around your timeline to get it done promptly.
