No pressure. No obligation. Just straightforward, professional advice.

What Our Survey Covers

During a lift survey, we assess:

  • Staircase width, length and clearances
  • Floor construction and structural considerations
  • Travel height and available space
  • Power supply requirements
  • Doorway positions and access routes
  • User mobility requirements and practical use

This ensures the correct lift is specified and prevents issues during installation.

Engineer-Led, Practical Advice

All surveys are carried out by qualified engineers — not sales representatives.

We will explain the most suitable lift options for your property, outline realistic costs and advise on any limitations or preparation works required.

If a repair or alternative solution is more appropriate than a new installation, we will advise accordingly.
