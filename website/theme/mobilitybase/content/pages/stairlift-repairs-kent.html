Common stairlift faults

  • Beeping or won’t move Often a charging problem: the lift isn’t parked on its charging point, or the power is off at the wall.
  • Stops part way Safety sensors on the footrest and carriage stop the lift if something touches them or is in the way.
  • Seat won’t move The seat must be fully swivelled back and locked, and the key switch turned on, before the lift will travel.
  • Remote not working Call-and-send remotes run on their own batteries, which need replacing from time to time.
  • Batteries worn out Stairlift batteries wear out over time. Slow or unreliable travel is a common sign.
  • Noisy or jerky travel Usually wear on the rail or drive parts, which is best looked at by an engineer.

Before you call: quick checks

  • Check the plug is in and switched on at the wall.
  • Make sure the lift is parked correctly on its charging point at the top or bottom of the stairs.
  • Check the key switch is on, and turn it off and on again.
  • Make sure the seat is swivelled fully back and the footrest is clear.
  • Try the controls on the arm as well as the remotes.

Stairlift repair FAQs

Do you repair stairlifts you didn’t install?

Yes. We repair all major makes, whoever installed them.

How much does a stairlift repair cost?

A call-out during office hours starts from £140 + VAT. Any parts are priced and agreed with you before they are fitted.

Why is my stairlift beeping?

A beeping stairlift is usually telling you it isn’t charging. Check it’s parked on its charging point and that the power is on at the wall. If it keeps beeping, call us.

Can you come out in the evening or at weekends?

Evening and weekend call-outs are for Silver and Gold plan customers, who get priority breakdown cover 24/7. Everyone else can book a weekday call-out.

Is it worth repairing an old stairlift?

Often it is. On older lifts, failure of a main part such as the motor or control board can make a repair uneconomical. If so, we’ll tell you honestly and talk you through the options, including reconditioned stairlifts from £1,400 + VAT fitted.
