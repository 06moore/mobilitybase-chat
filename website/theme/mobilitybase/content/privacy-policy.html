Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mobilitybase Ltd (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you use this website or contact us. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Contact: Mobilitybase Ltd, Unit 29, Branbridges Industrial Estate, Branbridges Road, East Peckham, Tonbridge, Kent, TN12 5HF. Telephone 0208 936 3060. Email info@mobilitybase.co.uk.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this website we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, postcode, the product or service you are interested in and any message you write. We also record the page the form was sent from and the date and time.

When you call, email or book a visit we keep a record of that contact and the information needed to carry out surveys, installations, servicing and repairs, including the address of the property and details of the equipment.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange surveys, quotes, installations, servicing and repairs (necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and to perform that contract).
  • To manage warranty and service plans, including renewal reminders (performance of a contract).
  • To keep records required for safety, insurance and tax purposes (legal obligation and legitimate interests).

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business — such as our website host, email provider and accounting software — and with manufacturers where needed to order parts or register a warranty. They may only use it on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted after 24 months. Customer and equipment records are kept for as long as we look after your lift and for up to 7 years afterwards for legal and accounting reasons.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Essential cookies may be set when staff log in to manage the website.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
