The Benefits of Staying Active for Mobility


Many people consider stairlifts or home lifts because they want to stay independent at home. But staying mobile outside the home is just as important. Sports like bowls provide


  • ✅ Better balance and coordination helping to reduce the risk of falls

  • ✅ Social connections a great way to meet people and stay engaged

  • ✅ Low impact exercise keeping joints and muscles active without strain

  • ✅ Mental stimulation playing bowls requires focus and strategy


If you or a loved one are looking for ways to stay independent a stairlift or home lift can make life easier inside the home while clubs like Paddock Wood Bowls Club help keep you active outside of it.
