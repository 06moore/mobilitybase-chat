# Content plan – first 12 weeks

Rhythm: **3 posts a week** on LinkedIn, **3–4** on Facebook (~15 minutes a week once vacancies flow, because vacancy posts are auto-drafted). Never publish a vacancy, employer name or logo without the employer's permission.

| Day | Theme | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **New vacancies round-up** (all Featured/Premium + new Standard ads that week) | Social queue |
| Wed | **Career insight** (rotate pillars below) | Templates |
| Fri | **Individual vacancy** (Premium adverts get their own post – a package commitment) | Social queue |
| Monthly | **Employer introduction** (with permission) / **industry recruitment update** | Templates |

## Content pillars

1. **Vacancy announcements** – auto-drafted on publish.
2. **Engineering careers** – "What does a stairlift service engineer actually do?", "From electrician to lift engineer", "What is LOLER and why inspectors are in demand", "A day on a curved stairlift install".
3. **Industry recruitment updates** – salary ranges seen on the board (only once there's enough real data), regional hotspots, skills employers ask for most (from your own listings).
4. **Employer introductions** – short profile of an advertiser, written with and approved by them.
5. **Trainees & apprenticeships** – how to get into the industry, transferable backgrounds (electrical, appliance repair, automotive, military), any live trainee roles.

## Weeks 1–4 (pre-launch & launch – before many live vacancies)

1. Launch post: "Introducing Lift and Mobility Careers" (template A)
2. Sector explainer: stairlift engineering
3. "Why every advert shows a salary"
4. Sector explainer: home & through-floor lifts
5. Employer post: "Recruiting engineers? First advert free for selected employers" (only to followers – not a cold email)
6. LOLER explainer
7. Career changers: "Electricians – your skills transfer"
8. Sector explainer: ceiling track hoists & patient handling
9–12. Vacancy round-ups as listings arrive; trainees post; platform lifts explainer.

Weeks 5–12: follow the weekly rhythm; one employer introduction a month; one "what employers are asking for" post a month based on your own data.

## Measuring

Use UTM-tagged links (the auto-drafts already include `utm_source=linkedin|facebook`). Check the weekly report's vacancy views and apply clicks; keep what works.
