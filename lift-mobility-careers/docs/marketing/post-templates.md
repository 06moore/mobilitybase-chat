# Reusable social post templates

Replace `{…}`. Keep vacancy posts factual; always link to the vacancy page (not the employer's site) so views and clicks are counted.

> Introducing Lift and Mobility Careers 🔧
> A UK job board built for one industry: stairlifts, home lifts, platform lifts, hoists and accessibility equipment.
> ✔ Salary on every advert ✔ Apply direct to the employer ✔ Free for candidates, no sign-up
> Engineers – browse roles: {site}/jobs/ · Employers – advertise from £39: {site}/advertise-a-vacancy/
> #EngineeringJobs #Stairlifts #LiftEngineer #UKJobs
