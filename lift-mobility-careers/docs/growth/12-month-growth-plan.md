# 12-month organic growth plan

**Principle:** organic first. Google search, Google for Jobs, free aggregators, employer sharing, LinkedIn/Facebook and job alerts. No general paid advertising. Optional micro-budget tests (≤£50/month) only after month 6, and only for job alert sign-ups if organic is behind.

## The growth loop

More live vacancies → more indexed job pages and Google for Jobs listings → more engineers visiting → more job alert subscribers and returning visitors → more applications per advert → employers see results, renew and share (backlinks) → stronger rankings → more employers want to advertise.

**Vacancy volume is the engine.** Early months focus on getting 15–30 genuine live vacancies through free introductory listings and permission-based featuring, *before* expecting traffic.

These are planning assumptions for a new domain in a niche UK market, not forecasts. They assume steady weekly outreach, one new guide per month from month 2, and that most new sites see little Google traffic until months 3–6. **Ranges:** conservative ≈ 60% of these figures; stretch ≈ 150%. If month-6 figures sit near the conservative line, change tactics (more vacancies via discovery, more employer sharing) rather than waiting.

**Other month-12 goals:** search engines ≥50% of visits; Apply-click rate ≥8% of job views; ≥10 verified employer backlinks; page 1 in Google for at least 5 of the primary sector/role terms (check in Search Console); 50–100 employers in the CRM with a relationship.

**Reasoning check:** ~70 live vacancies × ~85 views each ≈ 6,000 job views. Paying employers: ~28 of a 50–100 target relationship list converting after their free listing is realistic but depends on results – the share kit, views/apply stats in the dashboard and expiry reminders are the levers.

## Quarter by quarter

**Q1 (months 1–3) – foundations & supply**
- Launch with Search Console, sitemap, Indexing API and IndexNow connected; submit the job feed to aggregators.
- Outreach: verify 40 employers; offer free listings to 20–30; feature authorised discovered vacancies.
- Social: create pages, post launch, 3 posts/week.
- Content: publish 1 new guide (month 2–3).
- KPI focus: live vacancies, indexed pages, first subscribers.

**Q2 (months 4–6) – first organic traction**
- Watch Search Console weekly for queries appearing; improve titles/copy for striking-distance terms.
- Ask every advertiser to use their share kit; aim for the first verified backlinks.
- Promote job alerts in LinkedIn/Facebook groups (where rules allow) and from guides.
- Approach colleges/resettlement organisations with the career guides.
- KPI focus: organic share of visits, subscribers, first renewals.

**Q3 (months 7–9) – compounding**
- Location pages start indexing automatically as counties reach 3+ live jobs.
- Publish data-led content (what skills employers ask for; salary trends from our own adverts).
- Weekly vacancy round-up posts; employer spotlight monthly.
- KPI focus: returning visitors, apply rate, repeat advertisers.

**Q4 (months 10–12) – authority**
- Publish the first "UK mobility lift engineering jobs market" summary from 12 months of data – pitch to trade press and associations (link building).
- Review targets for year 2; consider candidate features only if they add value (e.g. saved searches) without collecting CVs.

## Weekly routine (~1 hour once running)
- Monday: read weekly report; approve vacancies; review discovered vacancies.
- 5–10 one-to-one outreach emails; follow-ups.
- Copy social drafts (3 posts).
- Monthly: read the monthly report; act on its suggested actions; publish one guide.

## Running cost impact
- Job alerts send email: size the SMTP plan to subscribers (e.g. 450 daily-digest subscribers ≈ up to ~10,000 emails/month in a busy month; most free tiers allow ~300/day, so a ~£10–20/month plan may be needed later in year 1). Weekly digests reduce volume.
- Everything else uses existing hosting – still within the £50–75/month target.
