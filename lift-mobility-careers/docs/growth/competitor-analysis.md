# Competitor check – who shows up for our main searches

**Date:** November 2026. **Method:** web searches for the core job searches. **Caveat:** the search tool available here is not the same as a UK Google results page (personalisation, location and Google for Jobs boxes differ). Treat this as a picture of *who is visible*, not exact rankings. Re-check in a private browser window on google.co.uk after launch, then rely on Search Console.

## What appears today

| Search | What dominates | Specialist presence |
|---|---|---|
| stairlift engineer jobs | **ZipRecruiter** aggregator pages, padded with unrelated engineering roles (refrigeration, gas, software) and headline counts of "14,000–28,000 stairlift engineer jobs" that are clearly inflated | None |
| home lift engineer jobs | Indeed browse page, Jobijoba, Jobbird, jobs24, DWP Find a Job, employer listings (Stannah, Stiltz) re-posted on aggregators | Recruiter ads (Marmon Lift Recruitment, Ex-Mil) |
| platform lift engineer jobs | Jobijoba, Jobbird, jobs.co.uk, EngineeringJobs.co.uk, Simply Hired | Recruiter ads (Stirling Warrington, REClifts, WR Engineering) |
| LOLER inspector jobs | Jobijoba, The Engineer jobs, inspection bodies (HSB, ISIS Safety, TÜV SÜD), SafetyCulture explainers | Mostly cranes/industrial – **almost nothing for stairlift/hoist LOLER** |
| ceiling hoist engineer jobs | DWP Find a Job, Jobijoba, JobLeads, Rise Technical, Millbrook/Medequip/TVMC ads | None |
| specialist mobility lift job board | Individual listings and one recruiter (Marmon Lift Recruitment) | **No dedicated job board found** |

## What this means

1. **The gap is real.** No site is the home of mobility lift engineering jobs. Searchers get generic aggregators full of irrelevant "lift" roles (passenger lifts, forklifts, cranes) and duplicated, expired ads. A clean, specialist, salary-shown board is a genuine improvement for both engineers and employers.
2. **Who we're really competing with for attention:**
   - **Generic aggregators** (ZipRecruiter, Jobijoba, Jobbird, jobs24, Indeed): huge authority, poor relevance. We beat them on relevance, freshness and specialist content, not on volume.
   - **Lift-specialist recruitment agencies** (Marmon Lift Recruitment, Rise Technical, Stirling Warrington, REClifts, Ex-Mil): they charge employers placement fees. Our pitch to employers is a fixed £39–99 advert with applications going direct, versus a percentage-of-salary fee. They're competitors for employer budget, **not** customers (we don't target agencies at launch).
   - **DWP Find a Job / Indeed:** free for employers. We win on audience quality and visibility. Many employers will still post there too, and that's fine.
3. **Easiest early wins (low competition):**
   - **LOLER inspector jobs for stairlifts, hoists and patient handling.** Current results are industrial/crane-focused. Our LOLER page and guide target the mobility niche specifically.
   - **Ceiling track hoist engineer jobs.** No specialist ranks; employers like Millbrook, Medequip, Arjo and TVMC recruit repeatedly.
   - **Trainee / career-change searches** ("how to become a stairlift engineer"): the results are thin. Our guides target these.
   - **Salary searches**, once the salary guide has real data.
4. **Harder:** the broad "stairlift engineer jobs" term. Aggregators have the authority, but their pages are low quality. Expect months 4–9 before we compete; Google for Jobs listings (our JobPosting data plus the Indexing API) can get vacancies seen sooner.
5. **Market signal:** demand for engineers is visible and steady. Stannah, Arjo, Acorn, Stiltz, Handicare, Millbrook and independents (Halton, Senior Stairlifts) all advertised in 2025–26. Typical pay is about £26k–£50k, LOLER and specialist roles higher. Employers repeatedly ask for driving licences, NVQ 2/3 and stairlift/hoist experience – our structured fields already capture these.

## Actions
- Lead outreach with employers who recruit repeatedly (Stannah, Arjo, Millbrook, Stiltz, Acorn, Handicare) and growing independents (Halton, Senior Stairlifts).
- Position against agencies: "Fixed price from £39. No placement fee. Applications straight to you."
- Prioritise the LOLER and ceiling-hoist landing pages and guides in early content work.
- Re-run this check monthly from Search Console data once live.
