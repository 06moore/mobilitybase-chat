## Recommendation
1. **Main site: liftandmobilitycareers.co.uk.** It matches the brand exactly, and .co.uk signals a UK business to UK searchers and employers.
2. **Also register liftandmobilitycareers.com and .uk** and redirect them to the .co.uk, so nobody else can take them. This is cheap brand protection.
3. **Optional: stairliftjobs.co.uk**, redirected to the stairlift jobs page. Exact-match domains give little SEO benefit today, but it's memorable on vans, flyers and in conversation. Only worth it if cheap.
4. Buy from a mainstream UK-friendly registrar (or through the host you choose). Put it in the **business's** name and email, turn on auto-renew, and enable registrar lock and 2FA.
5. Before buying, do the **UK trade mark search** (UKIPO) for "Lift and Mobility Careers" to make sure no-one has a conflicting mark.

Estimated cost: roughly £10–30 a year in total for .co.uk + .com + .uk at typical registrar prices (check current prices).
