# Privacy and Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** [lmc_business show=updated]

## Part A – Privacy {#privacy}

### 1. Who we are {#who-we-are}
[lmc_business show=operator_full] is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: [lmc_business show=contact_email]. ICO registration number: [lmc_business show=ico].

### 2. Candidates – we hold almost nothing {#candidates}
You can search and apply without an account. Applying takes you to the employer's own website or email, so we never receive your application or CV. The employer's privacy notice then applies.

### 3. Job alerts (optional) {#job-alerts}
- **What we hold:** your email address and the preferences you choose (sectors, job types, location and distance, frequency). If you enter a town or postcode, we convert it to approximate map coordinates using the postcodes.io service so we can match nearby jobs.
- **Why:** to send the alerts you asked for. Lawful basis: your consent, confirmed by clicking the link in our confirmation email.
- **Your control:** every alert has links to change your preferences or unsubscribe. Unsubscribing deletes your alert and email address. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 7 days.
- We never share subscriber details with employers.

### 4. Employers {#employers}
- **Account holders:** name, job title, organisation, organisation type, work email, phone (optional) and password (stored only in encrypted form). We use these to run your account, publish your adverts, send service emails (confirmation, reminders, expiry and renewal notices, receipts) and keep business records. Lawful basis: contract, and legal obligation for records.
- **Occasional emails about our advertising services:** only if you did not opt out when you registered. You can opt out in your dashboard or with the unsubscribe link in every such email. Lawful basis: legitimate interests and the "soft opt-in" under PECR.
- **Business contacts we research:** we keep a list of organisations in our industry and, where published, business contact details (for example a recruitment email address, or a hiring manager's name and role) so we can offer our service. Lawful basis: legitimate interests. Every contact is added by hand – we do not harvest email addresses automatically. We do not send marketing emails to sole traders or partnerships without their consent. Every email identifies us and includes an unsubscribe link, and anyone who opts out is permanently added to our "do not contact" list. Contacts with no ongoing relationship are deleted after 24 months.

### 5. Contact form {#contact}
Your name, email and message, used to reply to you. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.

### 6. Payments (when available) {#payments}
Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details; Stripe's privacy policy applies to the data it processes. We keep payment records (organisation, email, amount, date) for tax purposes.

### 7. Who we share data with {#sharing}
Only the service providers needed to run the Site: our hosting provider, [lmc_business show=hosting]; our email delivery provider, [lmc_business show=email_provider]; Stripe (once payments are switched on); postcodes.io (only the town or postcode you type); and Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies. Where a provider processes data outside the UK, appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum apply. We never sell personal data.

### 8. How long we keep it {#retention}
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Employer accounts | Until you close the account, then deleted (payment records excepted) |
| Payment records | 6 years (HMRC requirements) |
| Job alerts | Until you unsubscribe (deleted immediately); unconfirmed sign-ups 7 days |
| Researched business contacts | Deleted after 24 months with no relationship |
| "Do not contact" list | Indefinitely – email address only, so we can honour your request |
| Contact form messages | 2 years |
| Email sending log | 2 years |

### 9. Your rights {#rights}
You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to our using it (including for marketing, which we will always stop), or to restrict or transfer it. Email [lmc_business show=contact_email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

### 10. Children {#children}
The Site is intended for people of working age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

## Part B – Cookies and similar technologies {#cookies}

**Summary:** browsing and applying for jobs sets no cookies, and nothing is stored on your device unless you choose "Accept" in our cookie banner. Employers who sign in receive the strictly necessary sign-in cookies listed below. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page.

### Strictly necessary (no consent needed) {#necessary-cookies}
| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| wordpress_logged_in_*, wordpress_sec_* | Cookie | Keep employers signed in | Session, or 14 days with "Remember me" |
| wordpress_test_cookie | Cookie | Checks cookies work at sign-in | Session |
| lmc_consent | Local storage | Remembers your cookie banner choice | Until cleared |

### Analytics (only if you click "Accept") {#analytics-cookies}
| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| lmc_seen | Local storage | Counts returning visits; contains no personal information | Until cleared |
| _ga, _ga_* | Cookies | Google Analytics, only if we have switched it on | Up to 2 years |

If you click "Reject", none of these are set and Google Analytics does not load.

### Measurement without cookies {#measurement}
We count page views, visits, where visits come from (for example "Google"), vacancy views, Apply clicks and shares. Your browser sends only the page address, the referring website and, on vacancy pages, the vacancy number. We do not store IP addresses, set cookies or build profiles; figures are kept only as daily totals.

### Other websites and managing cookies {#managing-cookies}
When you click "Apply", a share button or a link to an employer, that website's own cookies and policies apply. Card payments happen on Stripe's secure checkout page. Use "Cookie settings" in the footer, or clear cookies and site data in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop employer sign-in working.
