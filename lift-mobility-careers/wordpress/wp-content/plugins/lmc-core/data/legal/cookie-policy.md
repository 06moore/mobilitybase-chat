## Summary

- **Browsing and applying for jobs sets no cookies at all**, and nothing is stored on your device unless you choose "Accept" in our cookie banner.
- Employers who sign in receive the strictly necessary sign-in cookies listed below.
- Analytics is optional and only switched on if you accept it. You can change your mind at any time using **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page.

## 1. Strictly necessary (no consent needed)

| Name | Type | Purpose | Set when | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| wordpress_logged_in_[random] | Cookie | Keeps an employer signed in | Employer signs in | Session, or 14 days with "Remember me" |
| wordpress_sec_[random] | Cookie | Secure sign-in token for the account area | Employer signs in | As above |
| wordpress_test_cookie | Cookie | Checks the browser accepts cookies at sign-in | Sign-in page visited | Session |
| lmc_consent | Local storage | Remembers your choice in the cookie banner, so we don't keep asking | You click Accept or Reject | Until you clear it or use Cookie settings |

## 2. Analytics (only if you click "Accept")

| Name | Type | Provider | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lmc_seen | Local storage | Us | Records the time of your last visit so we can count returning visitors. Contains no personal information. | Until cleared |
| _ga, _ga_[ID] | Cookies | Google Analytics (only if we have switched it on) | Measures how the site is used | Up to 2 years |

If you click **Reject**, none of these are set and Google Analytics is not loaded.

## 3. Measurement without cookies

We count page views, visits, where visits come from (for example "Google" or "LinkedIn"), vacancy views, "Apply" clicks and shares. Your browser sends a small message containing only the page address, the referring website and – on vacancy pages – the vacancy number. We do not store your IP address, set a cookie or create a profile of you. These figures are only kept as daily totals.

## 4. Other websites

When you click "Apply", share buttons or links to employers, you leave our site and that website's own cookie policy applies. Card payments happen on Stripe's secure checkout page, which uses Stripe's own cookies for fraud prevention.

## 5. Managing your choices

- Use **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page to change your analytics choice.
- You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop employer sign-in from working.
