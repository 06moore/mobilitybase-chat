We want every advertiser to be happy with the service. Our refund approach is simple:

- **Before your advert goes live:** if you change your mind after paying but before the advert is published, contact us and we will refund you in full.
- **If we decline your advert** (for example, because it doesn't meet our [Employer Advertising Agreement](/advertising-agreement/)), we will refund you in full.
- **Technical problems:** if a fault on our side means your advert was not visible for a significant part of the listing period, we will extend the listing or give a proportionate refund.
- **After your advert is live:** fees are not refundable because the service has been provided, including if you fill the role early. You can close the advert at any time from your dashboard.
- **Unused pack credits** are not refundable but remain usable for 12 months from purchase.
- **Adverts removed for breach** of the Advertising Agreement are not refunded.

Refunds are made to the original card through Stripe and normally arrive within 5–10 working days. To request a refund, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your company name and the vacancy title.
