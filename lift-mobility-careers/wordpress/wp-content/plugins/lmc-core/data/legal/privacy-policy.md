## Candidates

We have deliberately designed the Site so that candidates do not need to give us any personal data. You can search and apply without an account. When you apply, you go to the employer's own website or send an email from your own email account directly to the employer; we do not receive or store your application. The employer's own privacy notice then applies.

We count anonymous page views and "Apply" clicks for each vacancy so we can report performance to employers. These counts contain no personal data and use no cookies.

## Employers and employer contacts

**Account holders.** When you register we collect your name, job title, company, business type, work email, phone number (optional) and password (stored only in encrypted form). We use this to provide your account, publish your adverts, take payment, send service emails (such as confirmation, expiry reminders and renewal notices) and keep business records. Lawful basis: performance of a contract and, for records, legal obligation.

**Marketing to customers.** We may send you occasional emails about our advertising services. You can opt out when you register, in your dashboard, or with the unsubscribe link in every such email. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, with the "soft opt-in" under PECR.

**Business contacts we research.** We keep a list of businesses in the lift and mobility equipment sector and, where published, their business contact details (for example a careers@ address or the name and role of a recruiting manager), so that we can tell them about our service. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (developing our business by contacting relevant organisations). We do not send marketing emails to sole traders or partnerships without consent, every email identifies us and includes an unsubscribe link, and we keep a suppression list so that anyone who opts out is never contacted again.

> Complete and keep a Legitimate Interests Assessment for this activity (template in docs/legal/compliance-review.md). Article 14 UK GDPR requires this privacy information to be provided within one month of obtaining data from other sources – the first outreach email links to this policy.

**Contact form.** If you contact us we use your name, email and message to reply. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.

## How long we keep data

- Employer accounts: while the account is open, then up to 2 years.
- Payment and invoice records: 6 years (HMRC requirements).
- Researched business contacts: reviewed every 12 months; deleted if no relationship develops within 24 months.
- Suppression list: indefinitely, containing only the email address, so we can honour your request.
- Contact form messages: 2 years.
