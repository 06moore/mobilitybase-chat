## 1. Our services

1. We publish your vacancy advert on the Site for the listing period of the package you buy (currently 30 days), in the format described for that package (Standard, Featured or Premium).
2. Featured and Premium adverts receive the additional visibility described on our [pricing page](/advertise-a-vacancy/) at the time of purchase. We do not guarantee any number of views, clicks or applications.
3. We are an advertising service. We do not select, introduce or supply candidates, and you are solely responsible for all recruitment decisions.

## 2. Your adverts

You must ensure every advert:

1. relates to a genuine, current vacancy that you are authorised to advertise (see the [Vacancy Authorisation Declaration](/vacancy-authorisation/));
2. is accurate, not misleading and states a genuine salary or salary range;
3. complies with the law, including the Equality Act 2010 (no discriminatory requirements or wording unless a lawful exception applies), immigration and right-to-work rules, and the national minimum wage;
4. does not ask candidates to pay any fee or buy anything; and
5. relates to the lift, stairlift, hoist, patient handling or accessibility equipment sector.

We may edit adverts for formatting, decline, suspend or remove any advert that we reasonably believe breaches this Agreement, and hold first adverts for review before publication.

## 3. Prices and payment

1. Prices are shown on the Site and exclude VAT unless we state otherwise. If we are VAT registered, VAT is added at checkout.
2. Payment is taken in advance by card through Stripe. Your advert is published once payment (and any review) is complete.
3. Multi-advert pack credits can be used for 12 months from purchase and then expire. Free introductory credits are valid for 90 days. Credits have no cash value.
4. We may change prices at any time; changes do not affect adverts or credits already purchased.

## 4. Renewals and expiry

Adverts end automatically at the end of the listing period or your closing date. We will email reminders before expiry. Renewal is never automatic – you choose whether to renew and pay.
