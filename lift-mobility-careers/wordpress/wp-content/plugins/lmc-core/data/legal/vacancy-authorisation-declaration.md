# Vacancy Authorisation Declaration

> DRAFT – solicitor to approve. Employers accept this declaration (with their full name, date and time recorded) each time they submit or edit a vacancy.

**Version 1.0**

By submitting a vacancy to Lift and Mobility Careers, I confirm that:

1. I am employed by, or formally authorised to act for, the employer named in the advert, and I have authority to advertise this vacancy on its behalf.
2. The vacancy is genuine and open at the date of submission, and I will close or update the advert promptly if it is filled or withdrawn.
3. The information provided, including the salary, location and requirements, is accurate and not misleading.
4. The advert complies with the Equality Act 2010 and all other applicable laws, and candidates will not be asked to pay any fee.
5. Where I provide an application link or email address, it belongs to or is controlled by the employer (or its appointed recruitment system).
6. Where I upload a logo, the employer owns it or is licensed to use it, and authorises Lift and Mobility Careers to display it with the vacancy.
7. I accept the [Employer Advertising Agreement](/advertising-agreement/) and the [Employer Permission and Content Licence](/content-licence/).

The name of the person making this declaration, their account and the date and time are recorded with each vacancy.

## Vacancies featured on our initiative

Where Lift and Mobility Careers asks an employer for permission to feature a vacancy, we will only publish it after receiving written permission (email is sufficient) from an authorised person at the employer. We write our own short summary, link to the employer's own application page, and do not use the employer's logo unless separately authorised. We keep a copy of that permission.
